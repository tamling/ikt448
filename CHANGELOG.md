# Changelog

Script changes, chapter by chapter — including **merged contributions from
annotations and pull requests, credited by name/handle** (see
`CONTRIBUTING.md` and the *How to annotate* appendix).

## Autumn 2026 (script v1.0, 14 September 2026)

- Converted the full LaTeX script v1.0 (twelve sessions in four parts,
  preface, how-to-use, sources) to a Quarto book with the same layout as
  the GRC1100 and SKY2100 scripts: light/dark theme, Hypothesis annotation
  layer (private course group), 25 figures rebuilt from their TikZ sources,
  every table captioned and numbered, a consolidated reading list.
- The `\answer{}` texts of the *Questions from the reading* and *Questions
  for discussion* moved out of the chapters into the source-only `answers/`
  directory (never rendered), as in the other scripts; the preface sentence
  that promised answers in the chapters was adjusted accordingly.
- Added 4–5 interactive exercises (sort / order / pick, with a solution
  reveal and section reference) per chapter, drawn from the chapters' own
  tables and definitions.
